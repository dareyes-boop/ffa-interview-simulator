Contest-Day Physical Presence Checklist

Because audio alone cannot see your posture, verify these California FFA rubric standards before competition:

  • Official FFA Dress Uniform: Properly zipped jacket, official tie/scarf, black slacks/skirt, polished black shoes.
  • No Materials Allowed: Do not bring notes, portfolios, or extra resume copies into the room.
  • 3-Judge Eye Contact: Address the judge who asked the question, then naturally scan all 3 panelists.
  • Poise & Handshake: Offer a firm handshake, wait to be invited to sit, and thank the panel upon exiting.
